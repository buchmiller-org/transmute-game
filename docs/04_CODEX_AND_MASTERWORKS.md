# Transmute — The Grand Codex & Milestone Masterworks

## 1. Overview & Long-Term Objectives
To avoid the dead-end feeling of *"I reached Tier 8, now what?"*, **Transmute** centers its macroscopic progression around two complementary systems:

1. **The Grand Codex (Option B):** An expansive, illustrated encyclopedia of all alchemical substances that rewards permanent passive perks and allows content expansion in future updates.
2. **Milestone Masterworks (Option A):** Complex, multi-board capstone projects required to unlock new workshop rooms and advanced machinery.

---

## 2. The Grand Codex System

### Discovery Mechanics
* The first time a player merges or synthesizes an item tier, a **"New Discovery!"** banner appears.
* The item’s entry is permanently inked into the Codex.
* **Codex Rewards:**
  1. **Research / Insight Points:** Granted based on tier (Tier 1 = 1 pt $\to$ Tier 8 = 10 pts). Insight is the currency required to unlock new equipment and rooms.
  2. **Permanent Lab Perks:** Unlocking a full branch or milestone tiers activates passive laboratory perks.
  3. **Reagent Buy-Back:** Once an item is discovered in the Codex, the player can purchase an emergency duplicate of that item using **Gold/Shillings** directly from the Codex catalog.

## 3. Milestone Masterworks (Multi-Equipment Projects)

Masterworks are non-standard transmutations that cannot be spawned or merged on a single basic workbench. They require transporting high-tier items from different rooms into the **Transmutation Hearth** or specialized crucibles.

Completing a Masterwork marks a major milestone in the player's journey.

## 4. Expansion Architecture for Future Updates

Because the Codex is designed as an open catalog, adding future content requires zero refactoring of core gameplay loops:
* **New Chapters:** Update 1.1 can add *Chapter II: The Subterranean Fungi* with 8 new mushroom tiers and a new *Mycelium Incubator* equipment piece.
* **New Masterworks:** Higher-level masterworks can be appended to the list with unique cosmetic lab rewards or specialized sandbox modifiers.
